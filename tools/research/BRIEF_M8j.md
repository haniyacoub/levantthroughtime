# Milestone 8, round J brief: the missing side for contested records

Follow every rule and the output format in `BRIEF_M8h.md` (same folder) and the `subpath` patch field described in `BRIEF_M8i.md`:
honest verification levels, verbatim quotes under 40 words checked by script, no Wikipedia citations, no browser tools,
no sub-agents, no unauthorised copies linked, no local paths in notes, do not edit anything under
<project>. accessed: "2026-10-20".

**No casualty figures anywhere**: not in quotes, notes, titles or URLs you add. Choose quotes that state positions, dates,
decisions or findings without numbers of dead or wounded; if a page's address carries a casualty figure, omit the url and say why.

## The task
Each target in `m8j_targets.txt` (under your group heading) is a record marked `"contested"` whose sources come only from one
side, or from neither side. The project puts Israeli, Palestinian (or, for Syria, Lebanon, Egypt and Jordan, the Arab state
concerned) and international sources side by side. For each target:
1. Read the record in <project>/data (and its notes) to see which positions are already sourced.
2. Find a source from the missing side that gives that side's own account, position or data on the same subject:
   - **Israeli**: government and Knesset documents (also via the Wayback Machine, as gov.il blocks automated access), commissions
     of inquiry (e.g. the Kahan Commission), the IDF, the State Comptroller, Israeli universities and research institutes (e.g. INSS,
     the Israel Democracy Institute, the Meir Amit Intelligence and Terrorism Information Center, JCPA), Israeli scholars, Israeli press.
   - **Palestinian / Arab**: the PLO and Palestinian Authority (WAFA, NAD, PCBS), the Institute for Palestine Studies and the Journal of
     Palestine Studies, PalQuest, Palestinian and Arab universities and scholars, Palestinian human-rights organisations (Al-Haq, BADIL,
     PCHR), Arab governments (e.g. Syria's SANA, Lebanon's official sites, Egypt's SIS, Jordan's official sites), Arab press.
   For a population record, "the missing side" means a demographic estimate or analysis by a scholar or institution of that side.
3. Add the source with a quote, and propose a notes append (English only) that states that side's position in neutral words,
   attributed to the source ("According to …"; "The Israeli government's position was …"), next to the positions already there.
   Do not change the record's facts unless the new source gives evidence that contradicts them; then propose a change, quoting both.
4. If no source from the missing side can be opened, list the target under gaps with what you tried.

Output: `m8-j<N>.json` (e.g. m8-j1.json) in this research folder. Finish with a short report: counts, sources by side, proposed changes, gaps.
